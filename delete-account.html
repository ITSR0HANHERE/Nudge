Delete your Nudge account

This page explains how to delete your Nudge account and data. Nudge is provided by Rohan.

First: deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store app (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nudge Pro → Cancel) before deleting, or you will keep being charged.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nudge → Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and data.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked to choose your Google account once more to confirm it's you.

Option 2 — you no longer have the app

Email rohankardam0@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in (or include that email address) with the subject "Delete my Nudge account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your Nudge account (Google account email, name, profile photo URL and account ID held in Firebase Authentication).
  • On your phone: notification history, app priorities, settings and cached subscription status. (Uninstalling the app also removes this.)

What we may keep

  • Aggregated, non-identifying usage statistics from Firebase Analytics, until its retention period (up to 14 months) ends.
  • Payment records are held by Google Play, not by us.

See also our Privacy Policy.
