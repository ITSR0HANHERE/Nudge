Nudge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Nudge (the "app") is an Android app that helps you notice important notifications by ranking the notifications on your phone by priority, repeating reminders for important ones until you acknowledge them, and holding back the rest for a daily digest. The app is provided by YOUR-NAME ("we", "us"). Contact: YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL@example.com.

The short version

  • The content of your notifications never leaves your phone. Nudge reads it only on your device, stores it encrypted on your device, and never uploads, shares or sells it.
  • We use Google sign-in (via Firebase) to give you an account, and Firebase Analytics to count how many people use the app.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • You can delete your account and all your data inside the app, or by asking us.

1. Information the app handles on your device only

To do its job, Nudge uses Android's notification access permission, which you must switch on yourself. With it, the app can see notifications from your other apps: the app name, title, message text and time. This can include private messages and one-time codes.

  • It is used only to rank notifications by the priority you choose for each app, to show you reminders, and to build your daily digest.
  • It is stored in an encrypted database on your phone (the key is protected by the Android Keystore). Backups are disabled.
  • It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train any AI model.
  • You choose how long it is kept (7, 30 or 90 days), can delete all history at any time in Settings, and can revoke notification access at any time in Android settings.

The app also stores your settings (priorities per app, quiet hours, digest time) on your device. To list your apps so you can set priorities, the app asks Android which launcher apps are installed; this list also stays on your device.

3. Payments

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card or bank details. You can cancel in Google Play at any time (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Deleting your Nudge account does not cancel a paid subscription — cancel it in Google Play first.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account and data. This deletes your account from our systems and wipes everything Nudge stored on your phone. Or see how to request deletion without the app.
  • Delete notification history: Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all history.
  • Stop notification access: Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (Notification access) → turn Nudge off.
  • You may ask us to access, correct or erase the personal information we hold about you by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.

5. Children

Nudge is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Notification content is stored in an encrypted database with a key held in the Android Keystore. Account data is handled by Google Firebase, which encrypts data in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how Nudge handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date, and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
